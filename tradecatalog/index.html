02

who we are

maison maxine makes organic and clean red spritzes, produced by Gubel Family Vineyards in Napa. Whole ingredients, sweetened with honey.

“reimagining red wine for the modern muse”

the shopper

Women 21 to 35, especially Gen Z and younger millennials. They read labels, skip the sugar bombs and still want something that feels like wine. They buy for hosting, the beach and the patio, and they care about the story behind what they buy.

flavor one

organic sangria spritz

Organic Petite Syrah, orange, apple and lemon. 6% ABV, 250 mL can, no refined sugar.

flavor two

cab spritz

red wine x ginger ale. organic Cabernet, lemon and honey. 5% ABV, 250 mL can, no refined sugar.

New cans: one bold color per flavor and an oversized wordmark, so a facing reads as a single block of color in the cooler.
