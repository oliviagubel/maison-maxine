Maison Maxine FAQ

Everything you'd want to know about our Red Wine Spritz, in one place.

What is Maison Maxine?

Maison Maxine is a Napa Valley red wine spritz brand. Our Red Wine Spritz is Cabernet Sauvignon with ginger, lemon and honey, lightly sparkling, at 5% ABV, in 250 mL cans. It's for adults 21 and over.

What kind of wine is in it?

Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon. It's a red wine spritz, inspired by red wine and ginger ale.

What are the ingredients?

Four: Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon, natural ginger flavoring, lemon and honey. There's no refined sugar; the sweetness comes from the honey.

How much alcohol is in it?

5% alcohol by volume (ABV).

What size are the cans, and how is it sold?

Each can is 250 mL (8.4 fl oz). Online it comes two ways:

  • 8 cans in 2 gift boxes, $56
  • a case of 24 cans in 6 gift boxes, $144 (ships free)

Where can I buy it?

Online at maisonmaxine.world/shop, and at 17 shops in Southern California, New York City and the Hamptons, with more on the way. See the full list on our store locator.

Can I order it online?

Yes. We ship to 36 states and Washington, D.C. via UPS from Los Angeles. Shipping is free on orders over $100, and the case always ships free; under $100 it's $7.

Because it's wine, you must be 21 or older to order. We check your birthday at checkout, and an adult 21+ has to sign for every delivery. If your state isn't on our list yet, checkout will tell you.

How is it different from a traditional wine spritzer?

A traditional wine spritzer is wine topped up with soda water. Maison Maxine is Napa Valley Cabernet Sauvignon with ginger, lemon and honey, lightly sparkling and ready to drink from a 250 mL can. Best served chilled or over ice.
