the thesis

The biggest consumer brands of the last decade weren't built on products.
They were built on identity.

Alcohol is one of the largest consumer categories on earth — and it still doesn't have a brand Gen Z women see as an extension of themselves.

the problem

her whole identity is curated — except the drink in her hand.

01

Gen Z women are drinking less — and moving away from traditional red wine especially.

02

The beverage aisle hasn't evolved with them. It still speaks to their parents.

03

Her skincare, clothes, shoes, bag and home are all curated. The can in her hand isn't.

What you hold at a party is part of your personal brand — and nothing in the aisle is worthy of hers.

the insight

Gen Z doesn't just buy products.
They buy brands that match their values, their aesthetic, and their life.

The drink is no longer the point. The identity around it is.

the opportunity

Build the first identity-driven alcohol brand for Gen Z women.

Own the white space the entire category has left open.
