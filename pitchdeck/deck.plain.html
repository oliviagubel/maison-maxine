the problem

wine wasn't made for the way we live now.

01

Traditional wine is heavy, high-ABV, and chained to the bottle — the wrong format for the beach, the picnic, the 4pm spritz.

02

The "better-for-you" RTD aisle is a wall of hard seltzers that taste like nothing and mean nothing.

03

The modern muse wants something beautiful, low-intervention and social — with a brand she actually wants to be seen holding.
